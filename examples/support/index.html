Support panel

How to support a person or project, from plugins: Lightning (zaps and invoices, a browser wallet or a QR code), tip pages, on-chain Bitcoin and notes. Each entry is a field of the support-v1 manifest shown by its plugin.

WorldTrip's support page (inline)

The options of worldtrip.guide/support, given to the panel inline (.page): nothing is read from relays. Without an npub the Lightning entry pays with a plain invoice, not a zap.

Behind a button

<nostr-post-support-button> opens the same panel in a dialog, e.g. for a footer.

Anyone's support page (from relays)

A person's support page is one event of theirs (kind 30078, d-tag nostr-post-support). Load it by npub. Signed in with your own npub, you get "Set up your support page" (or Edit) to publish yours.
